Essentials

Essentials is an open-source Android app with 2.6k stars. I fixed a bug where battery widgets did not update when a charger was connected or removed. The cause was Android 8.0’s restriction on background broadcasts, so I moved the listener to a receiver registered at runtime. The fix was released in v20.3-beta.4.
